Why they struggle

The real reasons money disappears

  • Subscriptions auto-renew silently — Netflix, Spotify, cloud storage, forgotten trials that become monthly charges
  • Cash and card spending live in different mental buckets, never reconciled
  • Most budgeting apps require 45-minute weekly rituals that nobody actually does
  • Emotional spending (stress, boredom, social pressure) is invisible in spreadsheets
  • Banking apps show transactions, not patterns — they describe history, not behavior
  • No single place to see income, fixed costs, variable spend and savings in one honest view
